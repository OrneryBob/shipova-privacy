# Shipova Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 19, 2026

Shipova is an App Store screenshot design app for macOS made by Dylan Sola. This policy explains what Shipova does with your information. The short version: your projects and screenshots stay on your Mac, and Shipova does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.

## What Shipova stores, and where

Everything Shipova creates is kept locally on your Mac:

- Your current session and its recovery copies, inside the app's own sandboxed container
- Project files (`.shipova`) that you save, in the location you choose, including the screenshots you imported into them
- Images you export, in the folder you choose
- Your settings, such as recent projects, favorite designs and export preferences

These files never leave your Mac unless you choose to copy, share or upload them yourself. Shipova has no servers, no accounts, and no cloud sync. Exporting saves image files to a folder; Shipova does not upload them to App Store Connect or anywhere else. Uninstalling the app removes its container.

## Permissions

Shipova does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos library or screen. It can read and write only the files and folders you explicitly choose through an open or save dialog, or drop onto its window.

## Purchases

Shipova Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment and manages your subscription or purchase. Shipova receives only a confirmation from Apple that the purchase is valid, which it uses to unlock Pro features on your Mac. Shipova never sees your name, email address, payment details or Apple Account information.

Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your App Store account settings.

## Links to other websites

Shipova's Help menu, export screen and paywall contain links to websites, such as Apple's screenshot specifications, App Store Connect, the Terms of Use and this policy. These open in your web browser only when you click them. Shipova sends no information to those sites, and each site's own privacy policy applies once you are there.

## What Shipova does not do

- No analytics, usage tracking or telemetry
- No crash reporting services
- No advertising or ad identifiers
- No third-party SDKs
- No network connections, other than the App Store loading prices and checking your purchase
- No access to your files beyond the ones you explicitly open, import, drop or save

## Children

Shipova does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If Shipova ever changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. The current version is always available at this page.
